# Cropay

International card payment solutions for cross-border digital businesses.

Cropay is a payment gateway brand operated by DAYCHECKOUT NETWORK TECHNOLOGY CO., LIMITED, a Hong Kong-registered company. We focus on international card acquiring for cross-border digital businesses, including online dating, social networking, and live streaming platforms.

Our gateway supports payment integration with a merchant's own website or application. Merchants manage their products, customer relationships, subscriptions, and content. Our developer resources provide a starting point for technical teams evaluating how to connect their systems and handle transaction outcomes.

Visa and Mastercard can be enabled following approval through the standard merchant onboarding process. JCB acceptance is subject to a separate application and approval. We accept applications from companies registered in mainland China and Hong Kong. Companies registered elsewhere are assessed individually, and all merchants are subject to review before activation.

Settlement currencies and schedules depend on merchant requirements, the onboarding assessment, and contractual terms. Refund timing and charges depend on the relevant agreement and transaction. Contact our sales team to discuss eligibility, intended markets, and integration requirements.

Our website brings together product information, industry guides, developer documentation, and support contacts to help teams evaluate their international card payment requirements.
